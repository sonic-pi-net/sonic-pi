# Welcome friend :-)

Welcome friend. Today is the perfect day to learn how to live code!
{: .lead}

Hopefully you're as excited to get started coding your own sounds as I
am to show you. It's going to be a really *fun* ride where you'll learn
all about music, synthesis, programming, composition, performance and
more.

But wait, how rude of me! Let me introduce myself - I'm
[Sam Aaron](https://sam.aaron.name) the creator of Sonic Pi. I initially
designed Sonic Pi as an introductory teaching tool for Computer Science
in schools. However, whilst it's still great at that it has also
evolved into a powerful musical instrument that will let you express
yourself in new ways.

I believe that expressing yourself with code is the best way to learn
how to code. Sonic Pi was designed with this in mind at all times, so be
prepared to experiment, play and let the music that's in your heart out
through the code.

Watching others live code is a really great way to learn. I regularly
stream live on
[https://youtube.com/samaaron](https://youtube.com/samaaron) so please do
drop by, say hi and ask me lots of questions :-)

> **How this tutorial works**
>
> This tutorial is divided up into sections grouped by category. Whilst
> I've written it to have an easy learning progression from start to
> finish, feel very free just to dip in and out of sections as you see
> fit. If you feel that there's something missing, do let me know and
> I'll consider it for a future version.
>
> Also, if you have any thoughts, or ideas for improving Sonic Pi itself -
> please pass them on - feedback is so helpful. You never know, your idea
> might be the next big feature!

OK, let's get started...
