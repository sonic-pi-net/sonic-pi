Sonic Pi needs you

Sonic Pi is free, and always will be.

There is no big corporation behind it, no charity, no grants and no investors.

My continued work developing and maintaining Sonic Pi is paid for by people like you, one supporter at a time, through Patreon and GitHub Sponsors.

If Sonic Pi is useful to you, to your students or to your music, please support my work from the price of a coffee a month and help me towards financial stability.

— Sam Aaron
