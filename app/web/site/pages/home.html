Learn Code and Music Together

Rather than learning to code in a distant, abstract way, Sonic Pi lets you learn by making music.

Instead of sequencing, you write melodies. Instead of iteration, repeating rhythms. Instead of lists, bass lines and riffs.

The Sonic Pi Story

Initially a Computer Lab research project at Cambridge University in collaboration with Raspberry Pi.

Now independent, open source and community supported, Sonic Pi has been seen from school assemblies to the Royal Albert Hall, and all the way up to the International Space Station.

  • Convo at the Royal Albert Hall 1,000 young musicians playing traditional instruments, accompanied by a Sonic Pi quartet.
  • Codebus Africa Creative coding workshops for nearly 2,000 children across 10 African countries.
  • Rolling Stone at Moogfest Sam Aaron's live coded set at Moogfest 2016 “transcended the present”.
  • BBC Newsround Sonic Pi on the BBC's news programme for children.
  • Music Teacher Awards A finalist for the Awards for Excellence, alongside Boss and Korg.
  • Music in Space Students' Sonic Pi compositions played aboard the International Space Station by astronaut Tim Peake.

Sonic Pi has also won a Google Open Source award.
