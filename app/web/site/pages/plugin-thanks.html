{{icon download class="thanks-icon"}}Getting started

  1. Download the installer for macOS or Windows with the links in the licence email, and run it.
  2. Open your DAW and add Sonic Pi to a track.
  3. Paste your licence into the plugin. Pasting the whole email works too.

Keep the licence email. You will need it again if you move to a new computer, and its links always fetch the newest version.
